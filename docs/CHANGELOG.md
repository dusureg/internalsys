## 4.8 - Labels and receipts: labels of every kind in bulk, a receipt for every cost, a label designer, scan lookup

Data formats are compatible: 4.8 only **adds** one optional setting, `labelKinds` (see [DATA-FORMAT.md](DATA-FORMAT.md#compatibility)). It answers the note of 2 November: *scan the Production area for what is missing, make the label maker print many labels for parts, shipments and inventory with a QR code and a barcode, and make a receipt for every cost in the system.* The **pricing section moved from 4.8 to 4.9**, where it comes together with the contract paper designer (see [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md)).

- **Labels screen** (`#labels`, new **Production** group in the menu next to Progress; opens for anybody who may open Clients, Inventory, Logistics or Finance, and shows only the tabs they may use). Five tabs: **Projects, Parts, Inventory, Shipments, Receipts**. In each: pick the records (search, a filter, *select all shown*, *clear*), choose size, copies and layout, see the labels at once, and **print** or **save as pictures**.
- **Every label carries a QR code and a Code 128 barcode** of the record's own number: project `DS-KIT-2610-0001`, a part `<project>.<part>[.<piece>]`, an inventory item `MAT-0001`, a shipment `LG-0001` (`LG-0001.2` for line 2). The codes are always black on white, whatever the colours of the label, and carry **no secret**: only the internal number.
- **A lot of labels at once**: up to 400 labels in one go and up to 50 copies of each. Parts: one label for **every piece** of every part (a part of quantity 4 gives four labels, `k/n` on each), or one per part. Shipments: one per shipment or one per **line** (box or item). Inventory: one per item.
- **Sizes**: 25 x 25 mm, a strip (25 mm tall, 3 to 30 cm long), 50 x 30, 70 x 40, 100 x 60, 100 x 150 mm and **any size** from 15 to 300 mm. Text that does not fit is shrunk a step at a time; a label that still does not fit is marked on the screen.
- **Printing**: one label per page of exactly its size (label printer or *Save as PDF*), or **labels side by side on a sheet** (the paper size of Settings, 8 mm margin) for ordinary paper and sticker sheets.
- **Save as picture**: one label as a PNG named after its code, or all of them in **one ZIP** (no name twice), drawn at a sharp size and in the shape of the label.
- **Label designer** (a panel on every tab): five themes (light, dark, sand, green, sky), free **colours** for background, text and accent (a warning when the text cannot be read), **font**, **text size** 70 to 160 %, alignment, border (none, thin, thick, round), **which lines are printed and in what order** (up and down), and up to **six extra paragraphs** (own text with `{brand}`, `{code}`, `{date}` and the fields of the kind, bold, italic, three sizes, alignment). *Save as default* keeps the design of that kind in the sealed settings (needs the right to edit Settings; History records it); *Back to the saved design* drops changes.
- **Receipts for every cost** (`Receipts` tab): one printable receipt for **company expenses** (every repeat of a recurring expense), **shipment costs**, **payroll** (one per person and month, only for whoever may open Staff & pay) and the **cost lines of every project**. A receipt shows its number, date, what it is for, who was paid, category, cost kind (direct, indirect or not counted), the lines and the total, two **signature lines**, extra paragraphs, a QR code and a barcode of its number. Numbers are made from the record, never stored: `V-EXP-0007` (`V-EXP-0007-2611` for the November repeat), `V-LG-0003`, `V-PAY-2026-10-PPL-001`, `V-<project>-<line>`; the same cost always gives the same number. Paper: **A4, A5 or an 80 mm roll** (as tall as the longest receipt); period and sources are chosen above the list.
- **Find by code**: type or scan (a keyboard scanner types the code) any label or receipt code and the screen finds the record, opens it, or ticks it for printing. Unknown codes say so; a code for something the person may not open shows nothing about it.
- **On-demand loading**: the Labels screen is **not** part of the first visit of the admin site: `js/lazy.js` (a small loader, new) reads its seven files (three scripts of drawing and data, the screen, a style file and two dictionaries) the first time the screen is opened and keeps them. The first visit stays at **91 files and 361 KB gzip** (4.7: 90 files and 359 KB); the loader is also how the later large screens will be added without slowing the first visit.
- **Old Labels screen kept**: `#labels/<client>/<n>` (from a project and from Contracting) still opens the one-project screen of 4.6, with a link to the new Labels screen. The Settings > Labels card still sets the defaults of that screen.
- **Tests**: new suites `s41_labels_hub` (97 checks: bulk printing, QR codes decoded with OpenCV from printed PDFs and saved pictures, Code 128 bars read back, designer, receipts against the records, scan lookup, roles, Arabic) and `s42_audit_48` (19 checks: every tab, the designer, receipts and the menu in English and Arabic, dark and light, desktop and phone, with the keyboard); `s20_audit` now also checks that the Labels code is **not** read on the first visit. Full run: **1,573 / 1,573 checks (`run.py`), `boot.py` 24 / 24, the fonts test 24 / 24, all static checks**.
- **Found while testing (all fixed)**: the line "N labels" printed on a page of its own before the first label (every printed label page count was one too many); the designer showed a stray word "null" (an empty slot appended to a panel); editing one number in the designer reset the other paragraphs; the roll receipt was given the page height of a label; the receipt dates did not react to typing; "Select it for printing" did nothing when the screen was already on that tab; on a phone the A4 receipt preview was unreadably small (now real size, smaller only on phones); two names of helper functions clashed with the colour code of the client site (the check tool found it); a payroll table and a budget line were read before they were redrawn on a slow machine (three older tests now wait for the redraw; the QR reading in the tests also uses the second detector of OpenCV 5, which this machine has, because its first detector no longer reads the 100-pixel screenshot of the older label screen, on the 4.7 package too).
- **Production scan (what was found missing)**: *built now*: labels for parts, shipments and inventory; receipts for every cost; lookup by scanned code; one place for all of it (the Production group). *Not built, recommended next*: a cross-project **parts board** with status changes in one place (each project is saved separately and published to the client, so it needs a careful design); **dimensions and material on parts** (the cutting list of the Industrial Zone, 5.1); **work orders and routing** and **quality and defects** (two items of the industrial assessment that wait for your approval); **client-payment receipts** (money coming in) are not included here; **camera scanning** (the phone camera) is not built; the settings export and import do not carry `labelKinds`.
- **Not done on purpose / still open**: a **real label printer**, a **real barcode scanner**, the **phone camera** and a phone were not available: QR codes were decoded with OpenCV from the printed PDF and from the saved pictures, Code 128 was read back from the drawn bars with a decoder written for the tests, not by a scanner; very small labels (25 mm) are best read as **QR**; the "rest for every cost" request was read as *a receipt for every cost* (if you meant something else, say so); receipts are **not** legal tax invoices; a design is kept **per kind** and not per user; label designs are **not** part of the settings export.

## 4.7 - Control: sessions and shifts, staff and pay, costs into Finance, language fixes

Data formats are compatible: 4.7 only **adds** things (see [DATA-FORMAT.md](DATA-FORMAT.md#compatibility)). It answers the notes of 28 and 29 October and 31 October (the first part). The pricing section is planned for 4.8, the contract and label designer for 4.9, the Industrial Zone for 5.0 to 5.5; the full list, with what is done and what is not, is [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md).

- **Control** (`#control`, for the *technical* right and the administrator): **sessions and shifts per role**. For every role (and the administrator) set the minutes of inactivity before the session closes, a **shift**, whether the session **closes at the end of the shift** (with a grace time to answer "stay?"), and whether work **outside the shift is allowed or blocked**. Up to six shifts with English and Arabic names; the week days that count as work days (default Saturday to Thursday). **Feature switches** for suppliers, inventory, logistics, finance, collections, contracting, labels and staff (a switched-off section disappears from the menu and its address says it is switched off; the data is not touched); the upload limits for video and files (1 to 50 MB); how often a signed-in person is checked against the team list; the **address share links are built from** (a link is built from the address in use unless one is set); and a **repository connection test** (the repository and branch, whether the token may write, the data folders, the key file, the API calls left). Saved in `settings.control` and `settings.tech`. The default for a team member is 30 minutes of inactivity; the administrator keeps the device setting unless a value is set.
- **Sign-in records and the shift clock**: while somebody is signed in, this device keeps one sealed record for the month with when they came, when they were last seen (every 15 minutes by default, only while the page is open) and how the session ended (signed out, idle, end of shift, lost). A person writes **only their own file**. Near the end of a shift a dialog asks whether to stay; the time after the end counts as overtime; without an answer the session is closed. Work outside a shift that is set to *block* is refused, and the refusal is written in the History.
- **Staff & pay** (`#staff`, new section in the Administration group, right *staff*): four tabs. **Presence**: who is online now, who was last seen when, today's sessions. **People**: staff and casual workers with a rate **by the month, the day or the hour**, a start and end date, the cost kind (direct or indirect), an optional link to a team member, notes; one form each (`#people/<id>`). **Payroll** (`#staff/payroll/YYYY-MM`): one screen per month with base pay, **overtime** (1.5 times on a work day and 2 times on a rest day by default, per person if needed, rounded to 15 minutes, nothing below 15), **extra shifts**, absence and leave, **bonuses, deductions and advances**, the net, a **day sheet** for the whole crew, a person's entries under their row, **Fill from sign-in records** (turns the recorded sign-ins of linked people into hours and overtime) and **Close month** (a closed month is read-only). **Rules**: work days, days in a month (26) and hours in a day (8) used to turn a month into days and hours, the overtime multipliers, rounding and the minimum overtime, and whether payroll is counted in Finance. The pay is **never stored**; it is worked out from the entries and the rules, so the screen, the report and Finance agree to the cent.
- **Payroll report** (`#reports/payroll`): one line per person for a month with base, overtime, extras, bonuses, deductions, advances and net, totals, and the usual paper view, PDF, Excel, picture and share.
- **Costs into Finance**: (1) **payroll** reaches Finance as **one line per month and cost kind** ("Payroll, direct" / "Payroll, indirect"), never one line per person, so Finance readers see no names and no rates; the current month is dated today so it shows in "this month" at once; (2) a **shipment's cost** has a switch (*not counted / direct / indirect*); new shipments start as *direct*, existing ones stay *not counted* so no past figure changes; (3) **guest expenses** (`#expenses/new-guest`, "New guest expense"): hospitality, an indirect cost; (4) **discounts**: an expense can carry a *discount* (taken off the invoice), Finance counts the net. The ledger shows where a line comes from (badge and a source filter).
- **Client search fixed**: the suggestions on the client site appear while a number is typed (Arabic digits understood), spelling is matched loosely, and a partly typed phrase finds the record ("hello world" now finds a record only when the whole phrase is there; the older tests were changed to "hello world!").
- **Language conflicts**: Arabic words no longer appear in the English screens. History entries are stored without a language and are written in the language in use; the standard step names of a shipment follow the language in use, also for a shipment that was made while the screen was in Arabic; typed text (names, notes, clauses) is of course left as typed. A new test walks through the admin screens in English and fails on any Arabic word outside text the person typed.
- **Tests**: new suites `s36_control`, `s37_people_pay`, `s38_cost_feeds`, `s39_language`; the pay figures of `s37` are worked out by hand for the current month (8,000 a month, 50 an hour, 400 a day, overtime, advances, a bonus and a deduction). Older suites were updated where the behaviour changed on purpose: six roles in the team, the client search phrase, the first-visit budget of the admin site (365 KB gzip). `run.py` holds 1,456 checks (1,313 at 4.6), `boot.py` 24, `tests/tools/test_get_fonts.py` 24; the last full pass was 1,456 / 1,456 (and 24 / 24 for each of the other two). The new suite `s40_audit_47` audits the new screens with the rules of s20.
- **Found while testing (all fixed)**: a shift added in Control did not appear in the role lists until the screen was reopened; shipments made in Arabic kept Arabic step names in English mode (the field now follows the language until the person edits it) and the *Done:* button and the shipments report showed the stored name instead of the standard one; the payroll lines in Finance were dated at the end of the month and so were missing from "this month". The audit of the new screens found a stray word "null" in the payroll entry form and shift selects too narrow on a phone in Control (both fixed; see [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md)). The first visit of the admin site grew from 84 to 90 files and from 321 to 359 KB gzip (the budget was raised to 365 KB). Several checks were wrong and not the product (a collapsed `<details>` read as hidden text, a Finance tab that stayed open on the ledger, a no-break space in a money text, a person card toggled closed by a second click).
- **Not done on purpose / still open**: shifts and sessions are **rules of this application**, not of GitHub: anybody who holds the repository token can still write to the repository; shifts cannot cross midnight (no night shift yet); a person who is *not signed in* has no record; "last seen" needs the page open, and the times come from the **device clock**; the repository test and the pay figures were not run against a real GitHub repository; every "last seen" write is **one commit**, so a long working day adds commits (raise the minutes in Control to reduce them); the data folder stays `data/` (a different name is not supported); a **language check list** (content that is missing one language) is **not built** and moves to a later version; nothing here was tried on a real phone.

## 4.6 - Contracting, labels, brands and pictures, invoices, video and 3D files, down payment, formal contract copy, company overview

Data formats are compatible: 4.6 only **adds** things (see [DATA-FORMAT.md](DATA-FORMAT.md#compatibility)). It is the second of the releases that answer the notes of 19 and 28 October (4.7 control: shifts, sessions, IT settings, costs into Finance; 4.8 speed and PC design; 4.9 Business Hub and company finance; then equipment, other storage, automation, offline work and AI).

- **Contracts per project and a Contracting section** (`#contracting`, new report `#reports/contracts`): one line per project with contract number, client, price, wording, language, status (**draft, issued, signed**) and dates, filters and search. The contract screen of a project now has its own **wording**: the standard clauses of Settings, a **named template** (Settings > Contract: up to 12 templates with English and Arabic clauses) or **clauses written for that one project** (copy the standard or a template wording as a start, edit, clear). Contract data stays private; nothing of it reaches the client site.
- **Formal copy in black and white** (checkbox on the contract screen): the same contract with black text, a black band instead of the brand colour, white table headers and grey hairlines, the logo in grey; it is a *view and print option* (the print title ends with `-bw`), not a second document to keep in step.
- **Labels** (`#labels/<client>/<project>`, also from the project): small stickers 2.5 x 2.5 cm or a strip 2.5 cm tall and any length, for the project or for **every piece**; choose what is printed (logo, a thin Egypt flag with the slogan, client and product data, the piece, a **QR code** and/or a **Code 128 barcode** of the SKU). Every label is a page of exactly its size (`@page lbl`), so a label printer or *Save as PDF* gives one label per page. The settings (size, length, slogan, fields, copies) are kept in Settings > Labels.
- **Brands and pictures of products**: a list of brands in Settings (up to 80); each inventory item points at a brand and can have a **picture** (shrunk on the device; a 72 px thumbnail is kept in the record and shown in the inventory list, the full picture is sealed in its own file).
- **Invoices and files on records**: expenses and shipments take **invoice files** (pictures are shrunk, PDFs are kept as they are, up to about 780 KB sealed each). Files are sealed with the master key (`data/private/files/<id>.bin`), added to a record in memory, uploaded when the record is saved and removed after it. They are never published.
- **Video and a better viewer on the client site**: a project can keep a **video** in the repository (sealed like a photo, with a poster; up to 24 MB) or a **video link**; a direct file (mp4, webm) plays inside the page, YouTube, Vimeo and other links open on their own site. The viewer zooms pictures (buttons, double tap, + - 0), moves with the arrow keys or a swipe, and the zoom buttons start disabled at 100 %.
- **Files to download for the client**: drawings and **2D / 3D files** (GLB, GLTF, OBJ, STL, STEP, SKP, 3DM, 3DS, FBX, DWG, DXF, PDF, ZIP, images, TXT, CSV, XLSX, DOCX, PPTX; up to 24 MB each) kept sealed per project and **saved** by the client, never opened by the page; the list is part of the client's private snapshot.
- **Save our contact** on the client site: a contact card (`.vcf`, vCard 3.0) built from the public company profile (name, phone, WhatsApp, e-mail, website, address), so the client can add the company to the phone's contacts.
- **Down payment**: one row of a collection schedule can be flagged *Down payment* (only one per project). The client site shows **Down payment** and **Next payment** boxes (the next payment is not shown twice when the next row is the down payment), the Collections screen and the Collections report mark the row.
- **Company overview report** (`#reports/overview`, button on the dashboard, first in the Reports list): the whole company on a few pages for the owner: clients, projects in production, contract value, collected, still to collect, overdue, late projects and stock alerts; status and money charts, what falls due by month, progress of running projects, the list of every project with totals and a **Needs attention** list (late instalments, late projects, empty and low stock, late shipments). Paper view, *Save as PDF*, Excel and picture like every other report; private.
- **Tests**: new suites `s29_files_brands`, `s30_media_viewer`, `s31_downloads_contact`, `s32_contracting`, `s33_labels` (the QR code is decoded from the printed PDF with a real decoder; the Code 128 bars are checked structurally and decoded independently), `s34_addons` (down payment, formal copy, overview), `s35_audit_46` (every new screen and the client viewer audited with the rules of s20). `run.py` now holds 1,313 checks (1,022 at 4.5), `boot.py` 24, `tests/tools/test_get_fonts.py` 24; the last full pass was 1,313 / 1,313.
- **Found while testing (all fixed)**: the viewer opened a small picture at about 1,440 px and had its zoom-out button live at 100 % (sizes now follow the fitted picture, buttons start disabled); *own wording* of a contract replaced the object the text boxes were bound to, so typing after *copy wording* or *clear* changed nothing (the clauses are now changed in place); the report row counts included the total row; a long label strip was squeezed to the width of the screen (the label area now scrolls, the label keeps its size); on a 2.5 cm label a Code 128 barcode of a whole SKU is too fine to scan, so the **QR code was added** and the barcode kept as an option with a hint; label settings were stale after a change in Settings. **Changing a client's key stopped with a misleading "Not found (404)" when one sealed file of the project was no longer in the repository** (the key change read every file first): a missing file is now skipped and the save says how many could not be re-encrypted. On a phone the project tabs of the Labels screen were 29 px tall (under the 40 px rule) and are 40 px now. The checks themselves were fixed three times, where they were at fault and not the product: a stock test read a stale toast, the "no plain text in the History" check searched 4-letter words in random base64 text (a chance match is possible), and two new suites left dialog handlers behind that disturbed later suites. **"Print report" on the Contracting screen kept the client picked in an earlier report**, so the Contracts report could show only that client (or nothing): the company-wide reports (Contracts, Overview) now start from all clients.
- **Not done on purpose / still open**: the Code 128 barcode was **not** read by a real scanner (no decoder library could be installed in the sandbox); the QR code was decoded with OpenCV from the printed PDF at 300 and 203 dpi. A real label printer, the phone's real share sheet and contact import, and real playback of YouTube / Vimeo were not available. There is **no 3D preview** in the browser: 3D files are downloaded and opened in the client's own program. Videos and files above 24 MB are refused (the contents API of GitHub and a phone's memory are the limits); larger ones belong on a drive, which is planned for 5.1. The first visit of the admin site now loads **84 files** (the budget was 80; the speed work in 4.8 is meant to bring it down again).

## 4.5 - Everyday tools: owner sign-in, profiles, import, share, report pictures, paper sizes, look settings, internal codes, collections

Data formats are compatible: 4.5 only **adds** things (see [DATA-FORMAT.md](DATA-FORMAT.md#compatibility)). It is the first of four releases that answer the notes of 19 October (4.6 contracts, labels and brands; 4.7 offline work, staff status, HR and call centre; 4.8 Gemini and client traffic).

- **Administrator sign-in without the GitHub token** (Team screen, *Administrator sign-in* card): choose a user name and a long password and open the address the card shows on any device (*User name* tab): the administrator signs in with those two, like a team member, with full rights. The admin password and token methods keep working as before. The login file is public like every file of a public repository, so the password must be long (the screen enforces 12 characters and says why); remove the sign-in if a device is lost.
- **Profile** (`#account`, for every signed-in person): name, phone and a picture (cropped and shrunk on the device to 192 px, at most 60 KB). Sealed in `data/private/profiles/<id>.json`, shown in the menu and the Team list; each person writes only their own file.
- **Import from CSV, Excel (.xlsx) or JSON** into **Inventory, Suppliers, Shipments and Expenses** (`#import/<section>`, also an *Import* button on each of those screens). Columns are matched by name in English or Arabic (semicolon or comma separated, Arabic digits understood), you can fix the match, a **preview says what happens to each row** (add, update, skip, refuse and why) before anything is saved, bad rows download as a file, *add only* or *add and update* (matched by code, then name; empty cells keep the old value), and the whole file is **one saved change and one History entry**. A blank template can be downloaded for each section.
- **Share button** on every record and report: WhatsApp, e-mail, copy, the phone's own share sheet (when the device has one) and, for reports, the picture.
- **Reports as a picture (PNG)**, the width of the chosen paper, for a chat message.
- **PDFs fit the paper**: Settings > Paper size (A4, A3, Letter, Legal). Reports, contracts and labels print on that size; the test prints real PDFs of every report type and checks their size and that nothing overflows the page.
- **Settings: fonts, colours, settings file**. English and Arabic fonts (six and seven choices, loaded from Google Fonts, or the device's own), an accent colour that is **nudged until it is readable in both themes** (the contrast is shown), published to the client site too. **Settings file**: export the settings to a small JSON file and load it on another installation (company profile, business defaults, contract wording, codes, cost categories, look, paper; **never** passwords, tokens, keys, the inbox address or the project types); loading only fills the form, nothing changes until you press Save.
- **Internal codes** for material, supplier and shipment, built from a pattern you set in Settings (`MAT-{CAT}-{N:4}` gives MAT-BRD-0012; tokens `{CAT}`, `{YY}`, `{YYYY}`, `{MM}` and exactly one `{N:n}`, counted per prefix), given automatically to every new record, shown everywhere, searched, imported and printed, never changed once given; one button gives a code to the records that have none.
- **Units linked to the BOM**: a BOM line can name a material of the inventory; it takes the material's name, code, unit (piece, sheet, metre, m², kg, litre, set) and cost, shows what is in stock, and **Take from stock** books what the line still needs out of the inventory (the project remembers what was taken, so the same quantity is never taken twice; removing a line offers to put it back; the stock and the project are saved together and the History records both).
- **WhatsApp links you can change**: the button is still made from the phone number, but a client or supplier can have another number or a WhatsApp link (business message link, group invite) typed in; the address that will open is shown under the field; other sites are refused.
- **Client ID with the SKU**: every place that shows a project shows `DS-CL-1001/DS-KIT-2610-0001` (details tab with a copy button, progress, contract, dashboard); the SKU itself keeps its format and stays unique on its own.
- **Collections** (new): each project has a **collection schedule** (Payments tab): the dates and amounts the client agreed to pay, with a *split what is left into N monthly payments* helper; money received is matched to the instalments (a deposit that no instalment covers is taken off first, the rest goes to the earliest instalment), each row says *collected / part paid / N days left / overdue by N days*. **Collections** (`#collections`, Money group): every open instalment of every client, overdue first; tiles for overdue, next 7 days, next 30 days and balances with no date; filter and search; a **WhatsApp reminder** with the amount, project reference and date, and copy. The new report **Collections: instalments to collect** prints it (PDF, Excel, picture, share). The **client sees the next payment and the list** on the money card, and the contract paper has an **Agreed instalments** table.
- **Your own cost categories**: Settings > Cost categories adds direct and indirect categories (English and Arabic names); they appear in the cost lines of every project, the expense form, imports, Finance and the reports; removing one makes it read as *Other* and keeps the amounts.
- **Contact address** on the client site: the address of Settings > Company profile now shows in the contact box of the opened project as well (it was already on the search page), under the company name and above the call, WhatsApp and e-mail buttons.
- **Tests**: new suites `s22_owner`, `s23_codes_bom`, `s24_import`, `s25_look_paper_share`, `s26_collections`, `s27_cost_cats`, `s28_audit_new` (the screens of this release are audited with the same rules as the older ones). `run.py` now holds 1,022 checks (702 at 4.4), `boot.py` 24, `tools/test_get_fonts.py` 24; the last full pass was 1,022 / 1,022.
- **Found while testing (all fixed)**: a deposit was eating the first instalment of a schedule that listed only what was still to come (the allocation now takes the deposit off first); validating an instalment replaced the objects behind the rows on screen, so edits after a refused save were lost (the rows are now cleaned in place); the second language of a category was empty in lists when only one was typed; `brand.json` gained the optional `look` field (the whitelist test knows it); changing the unit of a BOM line did not refresh its stock link; removing a BOM line that had been taken from stock left the row on screen; accent text on its own tint and the client's *Pending* / *Overdue* schedule badges were just under 4.5:1 on the dark theme; the contract's *Open Settings* link was 22 px tall on a phone (see [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md)).
- **Not done on purpose / still open**: nothing here was tested against real WhatsApp, Google Fonts or a real phone share sheet (the sandbox has none): the links are checked for their address only; an imported `.xlsx` is read from the first sheet, formulas as their saved values; the administrator password sign-in is only as strong as the password because the login file is public (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)); Excel files with macros or password protection are refused; fonts are only downloaded in *Google Fonts* mode, the picture of a report uses the fonts of the device.

## 4.4 - Team and roles, History of every change, Finance (direct / indirect / total cost), organised Inventory table, ratings sent from the site, Troubleshooter

Data formats are compatible: 4.4 only **adds** things (see [DATA-FORMAT.md](DATA-FORMAT.md#compatibility)). A repository without the new files behaves like 4.3.

- **Team and roles** (`#team`, administrator only).
